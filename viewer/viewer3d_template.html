Drag to move the map (✋ — tap it for ⟳ and drag to rotate / tilt) · twist two fingers, right-drag or Shift+drag to rotate · pinch, wheel or +/− to zoom · ⟲ ⟳ ▲ ▼ N rotate, tilt, north up · 📏 measure: tap two points · arrow keys pan. Hover or tap a pin for its text. Shadows are exact at height ×1.
A terrain shared on Google Drive. Sign in with the Google account it is shared with to open it.
Tap the map where you want the 3-D view — the square is what you get: drag its orange corner (or Shift + mouse wheel) to change the radius, drag the red dot to move it — then build it. The square follows the model's UTM grid, so it is turned a little against north.
