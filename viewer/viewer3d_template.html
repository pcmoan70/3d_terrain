Terrain 3D — pick a place on the map

A viewer for 1 m terrain models (DTM) of Norway: pick a place on the map and the terrain is fetched from Kartverket, or open a folder with DTM GeoTIFF tiles (any UTM zone) and any .geojson / .kml / .kmz overlays.
